# GME UX decision note

Three inexpensive directions were compared before implementation.

| Direction | Strength | Rejected weakness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pressure chamber | Immediate drama and a strong central state | Encouraged gauges and a synthetic-pressure metaphor that could imply fake precision. |
| Squeeze ladder as the entire experience | Excellent five-second progression and next-state logic | Too linear to explain interacting mechanisms or countervailing supply. |
| Causal pressure field | Makes catalyst, market, options, short, borrow, and supply relationships inspectable | Required a secondary progression device for state transitions. |

The selected design combines the causal pressure field with a compact squeeze ladder. The first viewport answers state, change, why, next, and backward conditions. Deeper sections expose engines, competing explanations, capital structure, options concentrations, catalysts, freshness, history, and method.

The visual language uses amber only for the current pressure state, blue/cyan for observed evidence, dashed paths for inference, and red for opposing supply or falsification. It uses no decorative gauges, fake live motion, or ungrounded score.
